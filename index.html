About

I am a researcher in artificial intelligence and cybersecurity at the School of Computing, Mathematics and Physics, University of Portsmouth, working on the Horizon Europe XTRUST-6G project on Zero Trust Architecture for next-generation networks.

I hold an MSc in Cyber Security and Forensics (Distinction) from the University of Portsmouth and a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University, Bangladesh. My earlier research applied machine learning to healthcare and agriculture, including early detection of dementia and identification of medicinal plants.

Core development team · in production at universities across the UK and Europe

XTRUST-6G: Zero Trust Architecture for 6G networks

Horizon Europe · University of Portsmouth · 2024 to present

Research and engineering on Zero Trust Architecture for secure next-generation networks: continuous verification of users, devices and services, policy-driven access control and dynamic trust evaluation, as part of a European consortium.

DeepGuard: forensic detection of AI-generated images

MSc dissertation · 2024

A forensic model for distinguishing real from AI-generated images. DeepGuard combines zero-shot CLIP embeddings with handcrafted forensic features (pixel-level inconsistencies, PRNU sensor patterns) in a lightweight, explainable classifier suited to investigators and researchers.
