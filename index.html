About

I am a researcher in artificial intelligence and cybersecurity at the School of Computing, Mathematics and Physics, University of Portsmouth. My current work is on the Horizon Europe XTRUST-6G project, designing and implementing Zero Trust Architecture for next-generation 6G networks.

I hold an MSc in Cyber Security and Forensics (Distinction) from the University of Portsmouth and a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University, Bangladesh. In 2026 I was awarded the UK Global Talent Visa, endorsed by UK Research and Innovation (UKRI), in recognition of my research in AI and cybersecurity.

Alongside the 6G work I created and run CyberRange.world, a virtualisation lab platform for hands-on cybersecurity teaching. It is in production use by multiple universities and departments for their regular lab sessions and is expanding across the UK and Europe. I also research AI media forensics, including DeepGuard, a model for detecting AI-generated images. My earlier research applied machine learning to healthcare and agriculture, including early detection of dementia and identification of medicinal plants.

XTRUST-6G: Zero Trust Architecture for 6G networks

Horizon Europe · University of Portsmouth · 2024 to present

Research and engineering of a Zero Trust Architecture for secure 6G networks: a microservices-based trust plane built on Keycloak (identity), Envoy (policy enforcement), Open Policy Agent (policy decision) and custom SIEM analysis services that support continuous, dynamic trust evaluation.

Zero Trust6GKeycloakEnvoyOPASIEM

DeepGuard: forensic detection of AI-generated images

MSc dissertation · 2024

A forensic model for distinguishing real from AI-generated images. DeepGuard combines zero-shot CLIP embeddings with handcrafted forensic features (pixel-level inconsistencies, PRNU sensor patterns) in a lightweight, explainable classifier suited to investigators and researchers.
